About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Automatic Control at Leibniz University Hannover. My research focuses on the analysis and control of dynamical systems using data-driven and learning-based methods, with particular interest in nonlinear systems and biomedical applications. For more details, see my CV.

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of control theory, dynamical systems, and machine learning.

  • Analysis and control of nonlinear systems
  • Data- and learning-based analysis and control
  • Biomedical applications

For a detailed overview of these themes and related publications, see Research.
